The Slumber of Scorch-Scale

Dungeon Master's Guide: This mini-campaign is designed for three players (Levels 3-5). It uses D&D 2024 Rules.
Balance Adjustments: Encourage environmental interaction, allow "Heroic Recovery" (10-min Short Rest), and note that the final boss is weakened.

Introduction

Setting: Ignis Vale, a volcanic range where the air tastes of sulfur and ash.

The Conflict: The ancient Red Dragon Ignisvax was sealed centuries ago by the Monks of the Cooling Hand. Now, the Cinder-Caller cult, led by High Priestess Vora, has infiltrated the Temple of Sunken Embers to break the seals. If Ignisvax wakes, the nearby village of Oakhaven will be the first to burn.

Chapter 1: The Ash-Choked Ascent (Level 3)

Objective: Recover the Sapphire Orb and reach the Obsidian Gate.

Oakhaven is choking. Mayor Pendergast hires the party to find Kaelen, the only one who knows how to pass the Wall of Eternal Flame.

  • The Screaming Woods: Ambush by 3 Magmin. The trees "scream" due to wind in hollow trunks.
  • The Shrine of Frost: The party must defeat a corrupted Water Weird (Steam Mephit Swarm) to get the Sapphire Orb.
  • The Traitor: Elara Vane helps the party but secretly marks them to attract fire elementals.

Chapter 2: The Halls of Waking (Level 4)

Objective: Sabotage the three Focus Braziers.

The party infiltrates the temple. To weaken the dragon's awakening, three braziers must be extinguished.

  • Brazier of Logic: A puzzle involving the four seasons and directions.
  • Brazier of Shadows: A stealth encounter with a sleeping Hell Hound on crunchy charcoal.
  • Brazier of Souls: A moral choice to extinguish the fire (alert guards) or perform a ritual to free souls (defend for 3 rounds).

Chapter 3: The Heart of the Hearth (Level 5)

Objective: Defeat High Priestess Vora and Bind/Slay Ignisvax.

The ritual reaches its climax in the Lowest Caldera. Vora defends the dragon while chains snap every 2 rounds.

  • The Final Conflict: Fight High Priestess Vora. If the ritual completes, fight Weakened Ignisvax.
  • Choice A (Re-bind): Use the Ancient Hammers at the Forge Pillars to repair the chains (DC 15 Athletics).
  • Choice B (Slay): Kill the dragon for the "Dragon Slayer" title and rare loot.

Treasure Hoard

Sapphire Orb (Key of Cool Waters)
Wondrous Item, Rare (Requires Attunement)

A pulsing blue sphere forged from mountain tears. Grants Fire Resistance. Use charges to cast Fog Cloud or Lesser Restoration. Can be shattered to create a 30ft frost aura that extinguishes fires and suppresses dragon breath.

Hammer of the Cooling Hand
Warhammer, Rare (Requires Attunement)

An ancient forge-hammer. +1 bonus. Deals extra 1d8 cold damage to Fire/Dragon creatures. Grants Advantage on checks to repair the dragon's chains.

The Azure Savior
Cloak, Uncommon

Mayor Pendergast's "fireproof" cloak. Actually a modified Cloak of the Manta Ray. Grants water breathing and swim speed. Reduces the first instance of fire damage each day by 10.

Embershear
Dagger, Uncommon (Requires Attunement)

Recovered from the traitor Elara. +1 bonus. Can shed light and deal +1d4 fire damage. If the wielder has Skulker, they can extinguish it to Hide immediately.
